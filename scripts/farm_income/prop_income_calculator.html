Fewer lanes = faster cycle + more income, but higher variance (a bad streak hits a bigger stack). CV ≈ 0.34 + 0.95/√lanes (REFIT to the corrected fully-MAE MC; the old 0.26 + 0.51/√L was fit to the optimistic 43% model and badly understated risk). With the lower 34%/33% pass/payout rates, red years are now REAL at low lanes: P(losing year) ≈ 16% @2 lanes, 11% @3, 6% @5, 4% @10, 3% @15 (was a fake ~0.6% @5). The bad-year floor (p10) is negative at 2–3 lanes (−$16k @2, −$1k @3) and only turns solidly positive at ~5 lanes (+$6k). Beyond ~5–6 lanes the p10 floor barely rises (~$6–8k) while mean income keeps dropping — so ~5–6 lanes is still the sweet spot, now carrying an honest ~6% red-year risk. Go to 10 lanes only if you're red-year-averse (P(loss) 4.4%) and will accept ~$21k/yr less mean income. Source: lane_frontier_mae.py.
Formulas (audit the math)
copies = accounts / lanes  (one signal copied to all copies in a lane)
cycle_days = gamble_trades × (lanes / signals_day) + milk_days  ← the gamble is signal-limited; more lanes ⇒ each waits longer ⇒ longer cycle
cycles_per_year = trading_days_year / cycle_days
funded/cycle = accounts × eval_pass  (pass the eval gamble)
paying/cycle = funded/cycle × payout_rate  (survive the FUNDED gamble+milk to ≥1 payout)
$per_paying = $per_funded / payout_rate  (the avg over funded folds in the $0 blows)
gross/cycle = funded/cycle × $per_funded = paying/cycle × $per_paying   fees = accounts × eval_fee
net/cycle = gross − fees   annual = net/cycle × cycles_per_year
Two independent gates, not one. (1) eval_pass 34% = fraction of bought evals that pass. (2) payout_rate ~33% = fraction of FUNDED accounts that ever collect a payout. Both run the SAME MAE-aware $1500 1:1 gamble to +$3k, so the funded buffer-build reaches it at the same ~34% as the eval; milk (5 winning days) barely filters further → payout_rate ≈ eval_pass. So of 30 evals → ~10 funded → only ~3.4 ever pay. $per_funded (~$974, cap 2) is the average across ALL funded including the ~66% that blow at $0; the ~33% that DO pay average ~$2,970 each (≈$per_paying). It's below a single net payout because most funded accounts never reach one. (Earlier a bogus 43%/$1,363 came from scoring the funded gamble realized-only while the eval was MAE-aware — inconsistent; now both gamble AND milk are MAE-aware.) Milk intraday lows barely change it (+0.6% blow) — the gamble is the killer, not the milk. Payout cap varies by firm: cap 3 lifts $per_funded ~+27% ($974→$1,231 A, $1,019→$1,328 B). Per FUNDED, milk-down (B) > re-gamble (A); per YEAR, A > B (faster cycle). $per_funded already nets the 90% split and the 2-payout cap. Structure A re-gambles (faster, bigger payouts each round); B milks down (slower, safer). The throughput cap means buying MORE accounts past your signal capacity doesn't help — raise signals/day instead.
Pass rate (34%, 50k) is MAE-aware — the firm blows you the instant your floating equity (balance + open-trade MAE) touches the trailing floor, so a would-be winner that dips through a tight remaining-DD blows the account mid-trade. The old realized-only 43%/45% ignored this and overstated funded conversions ~20-25% (projections ~20% high at fixed evals, or need ~1.25× evals). Recover it with DD-aware buffer sizing: risk = clip(remDD − $900, $100, $1500) lifts a fresh account back to ~42% (avg trade MAE ≈ $900), at the cost of a longer cycle (size down when the cushion is thin).
