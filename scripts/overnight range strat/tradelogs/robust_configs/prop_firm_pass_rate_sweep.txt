HOW TO READ THIS
============================================================================================================================================================================================================================

- 'pass_rate' = fraction of historical starting points (in IS or OOS) where a 1-NQ trader starting
  the eval at that point would have reached +$3K before the $2K trailing DD breached.
- 'min_pass' = the WORSE of IS and OOS pass rates. Using the worse one to be robust.
- 'be_payout' = the per-pass payout needed for 5-account EV to break even, given that each account
  costs $100. At pass_rate p, EV(5 accts) = 5*p*payout - 500.  Solve for payout: payout >= 100/p.
- 'p5_atleast1' = P(at least 1 of 5 accounts passes) = 1 - (1-p)^5.
- For Apex/Topstep, first payout after passing the eval + funded account is usually $2-5K.
  Compare 'be_payout' to that to see your EV margin.
